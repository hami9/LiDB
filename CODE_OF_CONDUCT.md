# Community code of conduct

Our goal is a constructive, technically rigorous and accessible open-source project.

Contributors and maintainers must communicate respectfully, welcome good-faith discussion, avoid harassment or personal attacks, and respect privacy. Critique technical proposals and evidence rather than people. Do not share others' private information or secrets.

Maintainers may edit, remove or moderate abusive contributions and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations. Report concerns privately through repository maintainer contact channels where possible. Maintainers will seek a fair, proportionate response; this bootstrap project does not yet promise formal response times.
